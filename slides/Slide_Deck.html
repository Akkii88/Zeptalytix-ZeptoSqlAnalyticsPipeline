Zepto Inventory — Slide Deck

Executive Summary

  • Dataset: zepto_v2.csv (~3.7k SKUs after quick cleaning)
  • Objective: find merchandising priorities (stock risk, discounts, revenue opportunities)

Key Insights

  • High discounts (up to ~51%) concentrated in packaged snacks and dairy — verify promotions vs pricing errors.
  • High-MRP products (> ₹500) are sometimes out of stock — prioritize replenishment.
  • Price-per-gram analysis reveals value/product tiers useful for merchandising.

Suggested Actions

  1. Restock high-value out-of-stock SKUs.
  2. Investigate high-discount SKUs for margin impact.
  3. Tag products by price-per-gram for search and recommendations.
